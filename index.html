Engineering a More Accessible World

I thrive on the challenge of translating complex human needs into functional, reliable engineering solutions. I am passionate about applying my skills to solve complex problems through intentional mechatronic design.

I’m a Junior Engineering student at Harvey Mudd College specializing in mechatronics. My academic work focuses on mechanics, digital and electronic systems, controls, and human-centred design.

Beyond the classroom, you can always find me tinkering with my personal projects - whether it’s building a new electromechanical device or improving upon my previous designs, I’m always on the move to build highly reliable, real-world products.
